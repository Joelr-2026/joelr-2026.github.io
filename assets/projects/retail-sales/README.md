# Retail Sales Analysis using Excel

## Objective
To analyze retail sales data and identify key trends across products, categories, cities, customers, channels, and profitability using Microsoft Excel.

## Dataset Overview
- Total Orders: 1,200
- Total Customers: 478
- Total Quantity Sold: 4,252
- Total Sales: ₹10,926,608.33
- Total Profit: ₹3,275,893.37
- Profit Margin: 30.0%

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Excel Tables
- PivotTables
- PivotCharts
- Slicers
- Excel Formulas

## Data Cleaning
- Removed 5 duplicate rows
- Filled 6 blank City values with `Unknown`
- Checked date validity
- Checked quantity, discount, sales, cost, profit, and unit price ranges
- Verified text consistency across City, Category, Product, Channel, Payment Mode, and Returned fields
- Checked and confirmed no extra spaces in key text columns

## Key Insights
1. Fashion generated the highest sales of ₹2,936,782.56 and the highest profit of ₹875,025.58.
2. Backpack was the top-selling product with sales of ₹705,219.66.
3. Online sales were ₹5,638,053.06, slightly higher than Store sales of ₹5,288,555.27.
4. 317 out of 1,200 orders were returned, giving a return rate of approximately 26.4%.
5. Mumbai generated the highest sales among all cities.

## Business Recommendations
- Maintain strong inventory for high-performing Fashion products.
- Promote top-performing products such as Backpack.
- Continue strengthening the online sales channel.
- Analyze returned orders by product, category, and channel to reduce avoidable returns.
- Focus targeted marketing and inventory planning on strong locations such as Mumbai.
